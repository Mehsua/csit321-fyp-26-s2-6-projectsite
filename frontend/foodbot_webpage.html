What FoodBot does

More than a recipe search

FoodBot understands what you're working with — not just keywords, but ingredients, gaps, and dietary needs.

🥦

Ingredient matching

Type what's in your kitchen in plain language. FoodBot extracts the ingredients, confirms them, and finds recipes ranked by how well they match what you have.

🧮

Nutrition at a glance

Every recipe card surfaces calories, protein, carbs and fats — so you can cook with your health goals in mind without switching apps.

💬

Conversational cooking

Ask follow-ups, swap ingredients, request simpler versions, or find pairings — all in the same chat thread. No forms. No filters to navigate.

❤️

Favourites & history

Save up to 50 recipes to your personal favourites. Your dietary preferences and allergen settings are remembered across sessions.

⚠️

Allergen warnings

Set your allergen profile once. FoodBot flags recipes containing peanuts, shellfish, dairy, gluten, and more — before you ask.

📖

Step-by-step instructions

Tap any recipe to load full cooking steps with a missing ingredient checklist — so you know exactly what to pick up before you start.

The process

From fridge to table in four steps

No recipe books. No subscriptions to browse. Just describe what you have.

01
Describe your ingredients

Type naturally: "I've got chicken, garlic, and a lemon." FoodBot extracts the list for you.

02
Confirm or edit

Review the extracted ingredients, remove anything wrong, add what was missed — then confirm.

03
Browse matched recipes

See recipes ranked by match score. Allergen warnings and dietary badges are shown upfront.

04
Cook with confidence

Open any recipe for full instructions, nutrition info, and a checklist of what you're missing.
